# AttendX - Student User Guide

Welcome to AttendX! This guide will help you navigate your student dashboard and correctly register your attendance.

## Logging In
1. Navigate to the AttendX portal URL provided by your institution.
2. Login using your assigned institutional email and password.
3. Upon login, you will land on the **Student Dashboard**.

## Understanding Your Dashboard
Your dashboard provides real-time analytics on your academic standing:
- **Total Attendance**: Your aggregate attendance across all enrolled subjects.
- **Subject Breakdown**: A list of classes with individual percentage markers.
- **Recent Activity**: A chronological history of the sessions you recently attended.

> [!WARNING]
> If your attendance drops below 75% for any subject, a warning flag will appear next to it. If it drops below 60%, a critical notification will be triggered, and your Faculty advisor may be alerted.

## Scanning Attendance (QR Code)
When your professor opens an Attendance Session:
1. Click **Scan Attendance** from your sidebar.
2. A cryptographic QR Scanner will open, activating your device's camera.
3. Point your camera at the QR code displayed on the professor's screen or projector.
4. The scanner will automatically capture the token. Wait 1-2 seconds for the server to verify your enrollment.
5. If successful, you will see a green checkmark!

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: Can I scan a photo of the QR code that my friend sent me?**
A: No. The QR code cryptographic token expires and rotates every 10 seconds. By the time you receive the photo, it is already invalid.

**Q: My camera won't open!**
A: Ensure you have granted browser permissions for camera access. If you are on an iPhone, you must use Safari (iOS blocks third-party browsers from using WebRTC).
